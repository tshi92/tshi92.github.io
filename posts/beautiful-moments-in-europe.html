Beautiful Moments in Europe

Oct 2026 · Tuo Shi

A few moments I wanted to keep from travels across Europe, from the North Atlantic to the Mediterranean. More to come.

Finland·Estonia·Norway·Iceland·United Kingdom·Germany·France·Italy·Spain

Country

Finland

Helsinki

Summer in Helsinki: Uspenski Cathedral and the Natural History Museum.

And at Christmas: Senate Square, the Central Station, and Aleksanterinkatu.

Country

Estonia

Tallinn

Tallinn’s Old Town in summer, and the Alexander Nevsky Cathedral on Toompea.

Country

Norway

The Fjords

The fjords, seen from the ferry. I especially recommend Voss, a small town on the way to the fjords, where I found incomparable nature and a rare kind of quiet.

Country

Iceland

Iceland’s otherworldly landscapes left me in awe.

Reykjavík & the Golden Circle

Hallgrímskirkja in Reykjavík, then Brúarfoss and Strokkur on the Golden Circle.

The South Coast

Skógafoss, the cliffs near Vík, a glacier, and icebergs in the lagoon.

Country

United Kingdom

London

Big Ben and the Natural History Museum.

Edinburgh

Cherry blossoms in the Meadows, St Giles’ Cathedral on the Royal Mile, and the best pizza of my life at Mr Wood Fire. Highly recommended!

Country

Germany

Berlin

The World Clock on Alexanderplatz and the bust of Nefertiti.

Country

France

Paris

The Eiffel Tower at night and the Winged Victory in the Louvre, on the very day the Louvre staff went on strike 😅

Country

Italy

Rome

The Colosseum and the Trevi Fountain. Seeing the Colosseum still standing after nearly two thousand years was truly breathtaking.

Florence

The Duomo, and Dante’s death mask in the Palazzo Vecchio, where Dan Brown’s Inferno begins. I highly recommend his novels: Inferno (Florence and Venice), The Da Vinci Code (Paris and London), Angels & Demons (Rome), and Origin (Madrid and Barcelona). Part thriller, part travel guide to Europe.

Venice

A quiet side canal, and the gulls that stole my fried seafood cone.

Country

Spain

Barcelona

Inside the Sagrada Família, and the Temple of the Sacred Heart on Tibidabo, with its view over the hills.

Madrid

Puerta del Sol, the Almudena Cathedral, and the Royal Palace.

Toledo

The Puerta de Bisagra and Toledo Cathedral.

Segovia

The Monastery of El Parral, the Alcázar, and the Roman aqueduct.
